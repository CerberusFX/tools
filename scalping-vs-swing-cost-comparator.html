How this is calculated

Trades/month = trades per day × trading days per month, for each style.

Spread cost = trades × spread (pips) × pip value per lot × lot size. Spread is paid once per trade, built into entry price.

Commission cost = trades × commission per lot × lot size × 2 (round-turn: charged on both entry and exit).

This isolates the pure transaction-cost side of trading frequency — it doesn't model win rate, strategy edge, or holding-cost factors like swap. A strategy can still be profitable at either frequency; this just shows what volume alone costs before any edge is applied.
