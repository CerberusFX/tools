How this is calculated

Gain needed = drawdown ÷ (100 − drawdown) × 100. A 10% loss only needs an 11.1% gain to recover, but a 50% loss needs a full 100% gain — the relationship is not linear, it curves sharply as losses grow.

Periods to recover assumes your expected return compounds each period from the reduced balance: periods = log(recovery ratio) ÷ log(1 + return/100), rounded up. This is a simplified model — it assumes a constant average return with no further drawdowns along the way, which real trading rarely delivers exactly. Use it to understand the shape of the problem, not as a precise timeline.
