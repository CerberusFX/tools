How this is calculated

Forex: risk amount = balance × risk%. Stop distance in pips = |entry − stop| ÷ pip size. Lots = risk amount ÷ (stop pips × pip value per lot).

Stocks / Crypto / Indices: units = risk amount ÷ |entry − stop|. Position value = units × entry.

This assumes your stop actually fills at your stop price. In fast markets it may not — slippage and gaps can push your real loss beyond the number shown here. Spread and commission are not included.
