How this is calculated

CAC = (marketing spend + sales spend) ÷ new customers acquired in the same period.

LTV = average monthly revenue per customer × average lifespan (months) × gross margin %. This estimates the profit a customer generates over their relationship with you, not just raw revenue.

Payback period = CAC ÷ (monthly revenue per customer × gross margin %) — how many months until a new customer's margin has paid back what it cost to acquire them.

A commonly cited rule of thumb is an LTV:CAC ratio around 3:1 or higher for a healthy, sustainable acquisition model — though the right target varies a lot by industry, funding stage, and how fast you need to grow.
