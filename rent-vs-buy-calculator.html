How this is calculated

Buying: tracks home equity = home value (appreciating each year) minus remaining mortgage balance (amortizing monthly). Ongoing costs are mortgage payment, property tax, and maintenance.

Renting: the renter starts with the down payment + closing costs as invested capital. Each year, if buying costs more than renting that year, the difference is added to the investment and the whole portfolio grows at your assumed investment return.

This is a simplified model — it doesn't include closing costs on sale, tax deductions on mortgage interest, rental security deposits, or PMI. Use it to understand the shape of the tradeoff between building equity and investing the difference, not as a precise financial projection.
