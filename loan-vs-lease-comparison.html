How this is calculated

Loan total cost = down payment + (monthly payment × loan term), where monthly payment uses the standard amortization formula. At the end, you own the vehicle — this total cost buys an asset, not just usage.

Lease total cost = due at signing + (monthly payment × lease term) + end-of-lease fees. At the end, you own nothing — this total cost buys usage only, for a shorter period.

Effective $/month = total cost ÷ term, letting you compare options with different lengths on equal footing. This comparison ignores what the vehicle is worth at the end of a loan (resale value) — for a full picture, factor in that a paid-off loan leaves you with an asset worth something, while a lease leaves you with nothing to sell.
