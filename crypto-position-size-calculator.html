How this is calculated

Dollar risk = account size × risk %.

Stop distance % = |entry − stop| ÷ entry.

Position size ($) = dollar risk ÷ stop distance %. Wider stops (common in volatile crypto assets) automatically produce smaller position sizes for the same dollar risk — this is the same core logic as forex position sizing, just applied to crypto's typically larger price swings.

Volatility haircut is an optional extra reduction on top of the stop-based size, useful for assets that gap or wick further than their recent range suggests — essentially a manual safety margin for coins whose realized volatility exceeds what a single stop distance captures.
