How this is calculated

Hourly rate = annual salary ÷ (work hours per week × 48 working weeks/year).

Burdened rate = hourly rate × overhead multiplier, to roughly account for benefits, payroll taxes, and office overhead beyond base salary.

Meeting cost = burdened rate × attendees × (meeting length in minutes ÷ 60).

Annual cost multiplies this by how many times per week it recurs × 48 weeks. This is a rough estimate for decision-making, not precise payroll accounting — actual costs vary by benefits structure, contractor vs. employee status, and real hours worked per year.
