How this is calculated

Contribution margin = price per unit − variable cost per unit. This is what each sale contributes toward covering fixed costs.

Break-even units = fixed costs ÷ contribution margin. Sell fewer than this and you lose money; sell more and you're profitable.

Break-even revenue = break-even units × price per unit.

Units for target profit = (fixed costs + target profit) ÷ contribution margin.

This model assumes a linear cost structure — variable cost per unit stays constant regardless of volume. In reality, bulk discounts or capacity limits can change that at scale.
