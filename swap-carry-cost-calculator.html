How this is calculated

Swap per night = swap rate (pips) × pip value per lot × lot size, using the rate for your chosen direction (long or short).

Triple-swap day: most brokers charge 3 days of swap on one specific weekday (commonly Wednesday) to account for weekend rollover, since spot forex settles two business days later. This calculator adds 2 extra nights of swap when the "triple-swap day" flag is set to 1.

Total swap = swap per night × (nights held + 2 extra if triple-swap day applies).

Swap rates change with central bank interest rate differentials and can shift without notice — always confirm current rates in your broker's contract specifications before relying on this for a live position.
