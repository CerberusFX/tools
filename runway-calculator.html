How this is calculated

Net monthly burn = monthly expenses − expected monthly revenue. If revenue covers or exceeds expenses, net burn is zero or negative and runway becomes effectively unlimited (as long as that revenue continues).

Runway (months) = cash on hand ÷ net monthly burn.

Cash-out date projects forward from today using the current net burn rate. This is a straight-line estimate — it assumes expenses and revenue both stay constant, which real businesses rarely do exactly. Revisit this number regularly, especially after any material change to costs or income.
