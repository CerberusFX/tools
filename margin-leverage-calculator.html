How this is calculated

Position value = lot size × contract size × current price.

Required margin = position value ÷ leverage. Higher leverage means less margin tied up per position, but doesn't change your actual market exposure or risk — it only changes how much collateral the broker requires.

Margin level = (account balance ÷ used margin) × 100. Most brokers issue margin call warnings around 100% and force-close positions around 20-50%, though exact thresholds vary by broker — check your broker's specific policy.

Higher leverage lets you open bigger positions with less capital, but it doesn't reduce risk — it just changes how much room you have before a margin call on the same underlying position size.
