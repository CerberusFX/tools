How this is calculated

Each trade risks a fixed % of the CURRENT balance (not the starting balance), so wins and losses compound just like real fixed-fractional position sizing. Wins and losses are distributed evenly across the trade sequence at your stated win rate, rather than randomized — this shows the underlying compounding math clearly without random noise from any single simulated run.

Expectancy per trade = (win rate × reward R) − (loss rate × 1R), expressed in R multiples. Positive expectancy means the strategy should grow an account over many trades; negative expectancy means it should shrink, regardless of position sizing.

Real trading has randomness and streaks a smooth simulation doesn't capture — use this to understand the shape of compounding, not as a promise of a specific outcome.
