How this is calculated

Risk-adjusted APY = advertised APY discounted by your entered risk %, compounded monthly. The risk discount is a rough haircut meant to represent smart-contract risk, depeg risk, platform solvency risk, and any lock-up illiquidity — not a scientific probability, but a way to compare options on a more honest basis than headline APY alone.

Estimated earnings = amount × ((1 + monthly risk-adjusted rate)^months − 1).

A useful rule of thumb: a well-established, audited platform holding blue-chip stablecoins might warrant a 5-15% discount. A new, unaudited, or unusually high-APY protocol warrants a much larger discount — 30-60%+ isn't unreasonable for something offering yields far above the market average, since unusually high yield is often compensation for unusually high risk.
