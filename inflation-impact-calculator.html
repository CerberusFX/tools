How this is calculated

Future purchasing power = amount today ÷ (1 + inflation rate)^years. This shows what today's dollar amount will actually be able to buy in the future, expressed in today's dollars.

Return needed to keep pace equals the inflation rate itself — any investment or savings return below this rate means you're losing purchasing power in real terms, even while the nominal dollar amount grows.

This uses a constant inflation rate assumption for simplicity. Real inflation varies year to year and differs by what you're actually buying (housing, healthcare, and education have often outpaced general inflation figures) — use this for the general shape of the problem, not a precise forecast.
