How this is calculated

Kelly % = W − (1 − W) ÷ R, where W is win rate (as a decimal) and R is the ratio of average win to average loss. This gives the theoretically optimal fraction of capital to risk per trade to maximize long-term geometric growth.

Why half-Kelly, not full Kelly: full Kelly assumes your win rate and R are known exactly. In real trading, both are estimates from limited data — overestimate either one and full Kelly sizing can produce brutal drawdowns. Half-Kelly gives up some theoretical growth for a much smoother ride and more room for estimation error, which is why most practitioners use it or go even more conservative.

Negative Kelly means your edge (as entered) is negative — the math says not to take this bet at all, regardless of position size.
