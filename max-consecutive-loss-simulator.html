How this is calculated

This uses a standard probability recursion for runs in a sequence of independent trials: the chance that a streak of losses at least as long as your target occurs somewhere within your trade count, given your loss rate (100% − win rate) on each individual trade.

This assumes trades are independent and your win rate stays constant — real trading can have correlated outcomes (e.g. a bad news day causing several losses in a row for reasons beyond pure chance), so treat this as a baseline expectation, not a hard ceiling.

The key takeaway: losing streaks of meaningful length aren't rare edge cases at typical win rates — they're a near-certainty over enough trades. Position sizing and psychological preparation should account for this, not assume it won't happen to you.
