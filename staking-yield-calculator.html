How this is calculated

Effective APY = (1 + APR/100 ÷ n)^n − 1, where n is the number of compounding periods per year. More frequent compounding turns the same APR into a higher real APY.

Risk-adjusted APY applies your slashing/risk discount as a haircut on the nominal APY — a simple approximation, not a probability model. Real slashing events are rare but severe, so this is meant as a conservative planning number, not a precise forecast.

Lock-up period doesn't change the yield calculation directly, but it matters for liquidity: a locked position can't be exited if rates change or you need the funds, so factor that into your decision beyond the raw yield number.
