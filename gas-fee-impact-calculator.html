How this is calculated

Gas as % of trade = (gas fee × round-trip count) ÷ trade size × 100. The gas fee itself doesn't change with trade size, which is exactly why it hurts small trades disproportionately more.

Break-even move needed is the minimum price move required just to cover the gas cost on a round-trip trade, before any actual profit.

Monthly/annual totals = gas fee × round-trip count × transactions per month (× 12 for annual). Gas prices fluctuate significantly with network congestion — treat this as a planning estimate using your own typical fee, not a live quote.
