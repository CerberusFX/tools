How this is calculated

Max housing payment = (gross monthly income × DTI ratio) − other monthly debts.

Max principal & interest payment = max housing payment − estimated taxes/insurance.

Max loan amount is solved from the standard mortgage payment formula, working backward from that P&I payment, your interest rate, and loan term.

Max home price = max loan amount + down payment.

This is the "front-end + back-end" DTI approach lenders commonly use as a starting point. Actual approval depends on credit score, exact lender guidelines, other assets, and loan program — treat this as a planning estimate, not a pre-approval.
