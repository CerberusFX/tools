How this is calculated

Total cost = (hours per month × your hourly cost) + other direct costs (software, contractor fees, etc. tied to that client).

Monthly profit = monthly revenue from client − total cost.

Effective hourly rate = monthly revenue ÷ hours spent. This is the number that actually matters — a client paying a high invoice total but eating huge amounts of time can have a lower effective rate than a smaller client who barely needs support.

vs. target compares the effective rate to your stated target hourly rate, so you can spot which clients are quietly subsidized by the profitable ones.
