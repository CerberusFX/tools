How this is calculated

Break-even win rate = risk ÷ (reward + risk) × 100. This is the minimum win rate needed for a strategy to break even before any actual edge, at a given reward-to-risk ratio.

The relationship is inverse but not linear: doubling your reward-to-risk ratio doesn't halve the required win rate — going from 1:1 to 2:1 drops break-even from 50% to 33.3%, but going from 2:1 to 4:1 only drops it from 33.3% to 20%. Diminishing returns kick in as the ratio grows.

This calculation ignores spread, commission, and slippage — real transaction costs push the true break-even win rate slightly higher than the theoretical number shown here.
